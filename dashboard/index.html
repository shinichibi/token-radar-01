LAUNCHWATCH

A radar for tokens that do not exist yet — pre-token protocols ranked by what their launch would be worth, each with a plan to farm it.

How the board ranks

Implied value = TVL × peer multiple × momentum × maturity, computed from the snapshot's own figures, never from a list of names. The peer multiple is the median market-cap/TVL of traded protocols in the same line of business. Momentum is the protocol's own 7-day TVL change, clamped. Maturity adds 5% for every year the protocol has run with no token, up to eight years. A protocol joins the board when DefiLlama records no token for it (no CoinGecko id, no symbol) and it is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades — the same exclusions the snapshot's watchlist uses — and its TVL is at least $7.5M. Every watchlist protocol is on the board; the board reaches far below the watchlist's $50M floor. An implied value is a model's claim about a launch that may never happen, not a prediction.
